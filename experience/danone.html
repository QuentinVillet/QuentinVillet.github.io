Making photos safe to use

Consumer photos can show faces, licence plates, names and where they were taken. Under GDPR, the team couldn't use them as they were.

  • I built a pipeline on Databricks that removes metadata, finds personal information, blurs it, checks the result and writes a risk report.
  • I compared EasyOCR, PaddleOCR, docTR and Florence-2 on accuracy and speed before choosing one.
  1. Photo infrom consumers
  2. Remove metadataGPS, device, date

A simplified view. The code and photos stay with Danone.

Turning photos into insight

  • I used vision-language models on GPU to describe each photo and pull out products, brands, food, drinks and the setting.
  • Before building, I sat with the insights team to understand the questions they wanted answered. That changed what I built.
  • I presented the results to 200+ people across the global team.

What I took from it

A good model isn't enough. I had to weigh accuracy against GPU access, licences, cost and whether the team would actually use it.
